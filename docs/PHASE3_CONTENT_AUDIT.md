## Evidence and limits

All existing medical approvals remain in force: the user confirmed Dr. Vasundara’s review and service availability on 13 October 2026, recorded in PUBLICATION_APPROVAL.md. This is user-relayed review evidence, not a signed clinical record or an independent registration audit. Registration evidence and next-review deadlines remain missing. No new approval event or medical review date was created in Phase 3.

The current articles are concise overviews (159–178 narrative words before introduction, takeaways and FAQs), assembled from reviewed service copy. All 12 reuse their primary service narrative and FAQs. They are not comprehensive long-form articles. Reuse is disclosed editorially; substantive expansion and clinical differentiation require fresh review. Phase 3 preserves the narrative and improves its structure, attribution, navigation and sources.

Clinical wording was compared with authoritative public sources where accessible. Source availability, matching general claims and clinician approval are separate findings. A reputable reference does not substantiate every paragraph. Direct NICE/ACOG access was restricted in places; indexed NICE recommendations and primary ACOG publication abstracts were available. Full-text access/current reaffirmation remains a clinic task.
