## Scope decisions

WhatsApp remains the current conversion path because the user explicitly replaced the appointment buttons after Phase 3. The blank Google Forms setting remains blank; safe form validation is restored as an explicit optional path, not an unsolicited booking switch. Expert and patient videos retain click-to-load playback and are clearly distinguished. No VideoObject dates/durations/transcripts are invented. No llms.txt or AI-specific repetition is added.

Search readiness is not search visibility: the preview is intentionally noindex. Performance measurements, public-source access limits and clinic blockers are detailed in the associated reports. Stop after Phase 4; the Phase 5 checklist is preparation only.
