## Understanding the topic

Abnormal uterine bleeding describes bleeding that differs in amount, frequency, duration or timing from the expected pattern. The causes vary with age, pregnancy possibility, medicines and other health factors. Bleeding between periods or after menopause should be discussed with a clinician; symptoms alone cannot determine the cause.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Heavy or prolonged bleeding
- Bleeding between periods
- Bleeding after menopause

## When to seek medical advice

Seek prompt assessment for unexplained or persistent bleeding. Heavy bleeding with faintness or feeling very unwell requires urgent assessment.

## Evaluation and management

Document the bleeding pattern and discuss relevant history. Examination, blood tests or imaging may be considered according to symptoms and risk factors. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Management depends on the cause and personal priorities. Discuss medicine-related effects, procedural risks and alternatives when a treatment is proposed.
