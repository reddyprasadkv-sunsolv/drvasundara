## Questions for a first visit

- Suggest bringing relevant history, medicines, prior reports and questions.
- Do not invent local preparation requirements.

## Understanding tests and screening choices

- Organize questions around what a test assesses, why it is offered and what the result may mean.
- Respect informed choices and the limitations of screening.

## Planning contact and monitoring arrangements

- Ask who to contact for new symptoms and how review arrangements work.
- Do not publish clinic hours or emergency pathways before confirmation.

## Preparing for birth and postnatal follow-up

- Include questions about birth planning, follow-up and postnatal support.
- Distinguish general guidance from the actual plan agreed with the maternity team.
