## Understanding the topic

Antenatal care involves planned assessment and conversations during pregnancy. The schedule and investigations should reflect clinical guidance and individual needs, with attention to health history, screening choices, wellbeing and birth planning. A routine appointment schedule does not replace assessment when new concerning symptoms develop.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Preparing for a first antenatal visit
- Questions about pregnancy monitoring
- Reviewing test results

## When to seek medical advice

Discuss how to access timely pregnancy care. New concerning symptoms or reduced fetal movements need prompt assessment through appropriate maternity services.

## Evaluation and management

Review health history, pregnancy progress and indicated tests. Prepare questions about monitoring, birth planning and who to contact when concerns arise. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Tests and screening have limitations and may involve choices. Discuss the purpose and implications of each recommendation and avoid guarantees about birth outcomes.
