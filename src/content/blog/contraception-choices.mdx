## Understanding the topic

Contraceptive choices differ in how they are used, their duration, effectiveness, side effects and suitability for an individual. Reproductive intentions, medical history, medicines and preferences are relevant. Counselling should support an informed choice without pressure and clarify what protection, follow-up and return-to-fertility considerations apply.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Choosing or changing a method
- Side-effect questions
- Pregnancy planning

## When to seek medical advice

Seek advice when a method no longer meets your needs or causes concerns. Time-sensitive questions about possible pregnancy or emergency contraception need prompt professional advice.

## Evaluation and management

Compare suitable options, discuss practical use and consider medical eligibility. Clarify that pregnancy prevention and protection against sexually transmitted infections are separate questions. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Discuss relevant side effects, limitations and alternatives. Avoid presenting any method as the best choice for every individual.
