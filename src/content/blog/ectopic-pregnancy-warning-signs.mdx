## Understanding the topic

Most ectopic pregnancies occur in a fallopian tube, although other locations are possible. Early symptoms may overlap with other conditions, so pregnancy location requires clinical assessment. Pain, bleeding, shoulder-tip pain, dizziness or collapse in a possible pregnancy need appropriate urgent evaluation; do not wait for an online appointment response.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Pain or bleeding in possible early pregnancy
- A clinician’s concern about pregnancy location
- Follow-up after assessment

## When to seek medical advice

Severe abdominal pain, fainting or collapse in possible pregnancy requires emergency assessment. Do not use the appointment request form for an urgent concern.

## Evaluation and management

Assessment may use clinical history, ultrasound and pregnancy hormone measurements. Management depends on the findings and requires an individualized follow-up plan. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Observation, medicines or surgery may be considered in selected circumstances. Each has limitations; ongoing symptoms and follow-up requirements must be explained by the treating team.
