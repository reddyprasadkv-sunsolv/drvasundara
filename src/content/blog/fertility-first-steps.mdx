## Understanding the topic

Fertility assessment considers reproductive history, menstrual patterns, relevant health factors and the duration of trying to conceive. Evaluation may involve both partners where relevant; this does not establish that the clinic offers a separate male-factor service. The appropriate timing and sequence of investigations should be explained individually.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Questions about conceiving
- Planning after a relevant diagnosis
- Reviewing previous investigations

## When to seek medical advice

Ask for individual advice about when evaluation is appropriate. Timing depends on circumstances rather than an online promise or universal deadline.

## Evaluation and management

Review relevant reproductive history and discuss whether investigations or referrals are indicated. Evaluate the usefulness and limitations of previous results. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Investigations and treatments can have physical, emotional and financial implications. No test or consultation can guarantee pregnancy.
