## Understanding the topic

A high-risk designation indicates that closer assessment or additional specialist input may be appropriate because of maternal, pregnancy or fetal factors. It is a description of care needs rather than a prediction of a particular outcome. Risk assessment can change during pregnancy and should be explained in understandable terms.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- A clinician has recommended closer monitoring
- An existing health condition
- Concerns after a previous pregnancy

## When to seek medical advice

Do not delay urgent assessment for new concerning pregnancy symptoms while waiting for a routine appointment confirmation.

## Evaluation and management

Identify the reasons for closer assessment and coordinate care with the appropriate maternity team. The frequency of visits and investigations is individualized. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Additional monitoring cannot remove every risk. Discuss the uncertainties, available options and circumstances that may change the care plan.
