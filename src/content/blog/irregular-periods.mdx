## Understanding the topic

Menstrual concerns include cycles that are unusually irregular, absent, painful or heavy. A change may have several explanations, including life-stage changes, pregnancy, medicines or underlying conditions. Tracking the pattern can support a conversation, but online descriptions cannot identify the cause or determine whether an investigation is necessary.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Cycle changes
- Missed or infrequent periods
- Symptoms that disrupt daily activities

## When to seek medical advice

Discuss changes that persist or concern you. Sudden severe symptoms should not be managed by waiting for a routine consultation.

## Evaluation and management

A period diary can support the conversation. The clinician decides whether additional assessment is necessary rather than applying a single test to every menstrual concern. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

An explanation does not always mean that treatment is required. Where treatment is discussed, consider the goals, limitations and follow-up plan.
