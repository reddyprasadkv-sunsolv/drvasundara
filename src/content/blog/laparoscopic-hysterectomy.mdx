## Understanding the topic

Hysterectomy removes the uterus; whether the cervix, fallopian tubes or ovaries are also removed is a separate decision. The laparoscopic route uses a camera and small incisions. The indication, alternatives and reproductive implications should be clear before consent; ovary removal should not be implied automatically by the word hysterectomy.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- A recommendation to consider hysterectomy
- Questions about alternatives
- Understanding an operative plan

## When to seek medical advice

Arrange a consultation to discuss an elective operation. New severe symptoms need urgent assessment rather than waiting for surgical counselling.

## Evaluation and management

Clarify which organs would be removed, the reason for the recommended route and how the plan relates to reproductive goals and medical history. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Removal of the uterus prevents a future pregnancy carried in the uterus. Surgical, anaesthetic and recovery risks remain; alternatives and limitations must be discussed.
