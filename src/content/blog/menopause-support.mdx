## Understanding the topic

Menopause and the transition leading up to it can involve changes in cycles, temperature regulation, sleep, mood and genitourinary symptoms. Experiences vary widely. A review can also consider longer-term health and whether a symptom needs investigation rather than being attributed automatically to menopause.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Changes in periods
- Symptoms affecting sleep or comfort
- Questions about midlife health

## When to seek medical advice

Discuss symptoms that affect daily life. New bleeding after menopause needs clinical assessment rather than being assumed to be a normal transition.

## Evaluation and management

Review the symptom pattern, medicines and relevant medical history. Discuss suitable support options and whether further assessment is indicated. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Hormonal and non-hormonal approaches have different considerations. Benefits, contraindications and follow-up should be explained without promising a particular result.
