## Understanding the topic

An ovarian cyst is a fluid-filled structure in or on an ovary. Some are related to the menstrual cycle, while others have different causes. Management depends on symptoms, imaging characteristics, age and clinical context. A cyst finding does not automatically mean cancer or that surgery is required.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- An imaging report describing a cyst
- Pelvic discomfort
- Questions about follow-up

## When to seek medical advice

Sudden severe pelvic pain or pain with feeling very unwell needs urgent assessment. A scan finding should be interpreted alongside symptoms and individual risk factors.

## Evaluation and management

Review imaging findings, symptoms and life stage. The discussion may include observation, repeat assessment or further investigation when appropriate. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Not every cyst needs an operation. If surgery is considered, discuss its purpose, alternatives, possible complications and implications for ovarian tissue.
