## Understanding the topic

PCOS is a hormonal and metabolic condition with features that vary between individuals. Irregular cycles, androgen-related symptoms and ovarian appearances need interpretation together; an ultrasound finding alone does not establish a diagnosis. Care may address menstrual health, metabolic risk, symptoms and reproductive plans rather than a single universal treatment.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- Infrequent or unpredictable periods
- Skin or hair changes
- Questions about ovulation

## When to seek medical advice

Arrange a consultation for persistent menstrual changes or other concerns. A missed period can have more than one explanation and should not automatically be attributed to PCOS.

## Evaluation and management

Review the pattern of symptoms and consider other explanations. Testing is selected according to the individual; management discussions should reflect health priorities and pregnancy plans. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

There is no single approach suitable for everyone. Discuss medicine suitability and monitoring, and avoid promises of a cure or guaranteed pregnancy.
