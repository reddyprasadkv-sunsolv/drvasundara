## Understanding the topic

Robot-assisted surgery uses a surgical system controlled by the surgeon. The equipment does not perform an operation independently. Its suitability depends on the indication, complexity, surgeon experience and alternatives. Evidence, costs and procedure-specific limitations should be discussed without assuming that the technology produces superior outcomes for every patient.

## Reasons to seek an assessment

- A recommended robot-assisted procedure
- Questions about other surgical routes
- Understanding proposed costs and recovery

## When to seek medical advice

Discuss a planned operation with the treating surgeon. The choice of technique should follow assessment rather than marketing claims.

## Evaluation and management

Explain why a robot-assisted route is being considered, what alternatives exist and which factors influence suitability. Clarify the surgeon’s role and team arrangements. Questions about the purpose of a recommendation, reasonable alternatives and follow-up should be addressed before proceeding.

## Benefits, risks and limitations

Potential benefits and disadvantages depend on the procedure and circumstances. Discuss surgical risks, limitations and alternatives; do not imply universal superiority over laparoscopy.
